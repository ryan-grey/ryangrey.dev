What greyBot does

greyBot announces a World of Warcraft guild's first Heroic kill of each raid boss, announces Ahead of the Curve when a tier is cleared, posts an optional summary of a raid night, and answers a /progress command with the guild's current standing. It derives all of this from public raid logs and public guild profiles.

What it depends on

greyBot reads from Warcraft Logs, Raider.IO and Blizzard's public game data API. If any of those is unavailable, changes, or stops permitting this use, greyBot may stop working, in whole or in part. Guild data that is private to those services is invisible to greyBot, and it cannot announce what it cannot see.

Accuracy

greyBot reports what its sources report. Raid logs can be incomplete, tagged wrongly, or filed late, and progress data can lag behind a kill by hours. Announcements are best-effort statements about public data, not authoritative records, and they should not be treated as proof of anything.

Stopping

Remove greyBot from a server at any time through Server Settings → Integrations, or by deleting the webhook it posts through. No notice is needed and nothing is owed. To have a guild's stored data deleted as well, see the Privacy Policy.

Data

What greyBot stores, and what it deliberately does not, is set out in full in the Privacy Policy. In short: no Discord personal data, and World of Warcraft character names taken from logs that are already public.
