01the loading screen incident

spoot was born when a loading bar got to 99% and gave up. three pixels fell off, stuck together and grew eyes. no arms. no legs. just hims.

his first word was “retry.” his second word was also “retry.” rough start ha.

02briefly employed as a cursor

first job was pointing at things. should've been easy considering the shape. instead he kept hovering over “leave group” during boss pulls.

fired on day two. says it was a culture fit thing. it was not.

03scrambled takes him in

LEL found him living in an abandoned voice channel eating dropped packets. gave him a name, a pink paint job and one job: tell us when the boss dies.

hims did it. then someone said “can you also do signups” and that was the end of his work life balance.

04the soundboard changed him

one night somebody said something so stupid in voice that spoot tried to write it down. remembered he had no hands. invented an entire clipping system out of spite.

now it has a button. progress, apparently.
