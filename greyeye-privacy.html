greyEye

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

greyEye is an iPhone and iPad app that shows the live view of Ryan Grey's own Google Nest cameras to the people he gives a passcode to. It is operated by Ryan Grey. This page describes exactly what it stores, where the video goes, and how it uses Google account data.

The short version. greyEye has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It does not record or keep any video. The live picture goes straight from Google to your phone, and the only thing the app remembers is the passcode you typed.

What it stores

  • On your device — the passcode, in the iOS Keychain, so you do not have to type it again. Choosing Lock greyEye in the app deletes it.
  • On the greyEye server (Amazon Web Services) — the shared passcode, and the Google sign-in credentials for the camera owner's own account, both encrypted at rest. Nothing about the people using the app is stored there.
  • Operational logs — short error records such as a request path and a status code, used to fix problems and deleted after 14 days. They contain no video, no passcodes and no Google credentials.

What it does not store

  • Video, still images or audio. The app plays the live stream and keeps nothing.
  • Your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information. greyEye never asks for them.
  • Microphone input. The app never records sound; camera audio is muted.

Where the video goes

When you open greyEye, the server asks Google for a live stream on your phone's behalf and hands your phone the connection details. The video itself then travels directly from Google to your device over an encrypted WebRTC connection; it never passes through the greyEye server. Streams stop when you leave the app. Google processes the camera video under the Google Privacy Policy.

Google user data

greyEye connects to exactly one Google account: the camera owner's. It uses Google's Smart Device Management API (the sdm.service scope) only to list that account's cameras and to open, extend and stop their live streams. That data is used only to show the live view in the app. It is never sold, never shared with anyone else, never used for advertising, and never used to train models.

greyEye's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The camera owner can revoke greyEye's access at any time from Google Nest partner connections.

Who can see it

Only people who have the current passcode can see the cameras. The camera owner can change the passcode at any time, which signs everyone out within a few minutes.

Children

greyEye does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If what the app stores changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
